Threat: Phishing

Description:
Phishing is a social engineering technique used to trick users into revealing sensitive information or interacting with malicious content.

Common Indicators:
- Suspicious sender address
- Unexpected links or attachments
- Urgent or threatening language
- Requests for passwords or sensitive information
- Mismatched or suspicious URLs
- Unexpected login pages

Risk:
Phishing can lead to credential theft, malware infection, account compromise, and data loss.

Defensive Measures:
- Verify the sender before responding
- Avoid suspicious links and attachments
- Use multi-factor authentication
- Check URLs carefully
- Report suspicious messages
- Use email security controls

Investigation Steps:
1. Identify the sender.
2. Inspect links and attachments safely.
3. Check whether credentials were entered.
4. Determine whether the user interacted with the message.
5. Review related authentication and endpoint logs.
